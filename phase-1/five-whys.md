# Five Whys

**Surface request:** "Make the estimated material specs real, not estimated."

1. **Why?** Because the specs (transmissivity, mass, cost, span) for the 6 atlas-added materials are invented, not sourced.
   → *Why does that matter?*
2. Because the app computes cooling, carbon, and cost rankings directly from those specs.
   → *Why does that matter?*
3. Because the organiser sees those numbers and uses them to choose a shade material and to justify the plan to others (city, sponsor, safety).
   → *Why does that matter?*
4. Because a justification built on fabricated numbers collapses the moment anyone audits it — and the project's whole thesis is "honest, defensible, climate-aware design" (no greenwash, label every assumption).
   → *Why does that matter?*
5. Because the tool's credibility (and the professor's/owner's trust in it) depends on every claim being traceable to a real source or honestly flagged as an estimate.

**Terminal action:** Source each spec from a real datasheet/EPD and attach a provenance tier to every field; drop any archetype that can't be grounded as a real shade product.

**Decision-statement check:** consistent — no reframe needed. The decision (rank reclaimed materials for shade, defensibly) is unchanged; this phase fixes its inputs.
