A real Deep-RL pedestrian model that produces the crowd-density field — the demand half of the crowd×heat coupling that decides where shade goes.

834×918m · real Parc del Fòrum
PPOStable-Baselines3, seed 1789

01What it has to produce

A per-cell crowd_density field, snapped exactly to the UTCI heat lattice (6 m cells), so the greedy/MCLP core can multiply density × (UTCI − 26) and find where the crowd is hottest and busiest.

  • density per cell — on one documented scale
  • dwell_zones — where people stand still (DBSCAN over near-zero-velocity cells)
  • flow_vectors — the invisible highways between gates and stages

Hard rule: the model runs inference only on the pipeline. Training is a one-time, off-path regeneration script.

02The core idea: one shared brain, N bodies

Decentralised, shared-policy crowd RL (CADRL-style). One small MLP policy. Every agent observes its own local world and the same policy maps that to its action.

This is the only design where the pedestrian fundamental diagram (speed falls with density) can physically emerge — each agent must individually slow when its own neighbourhood jams.

SB3 trick

SB3 trains one ego agent (single-agent gym contract) among a reactive moving crowd. At runtime the same learned policy is applied per-agent to all 650 via step_all → the emergent congestion response fires.

03What an agent sees & does

Observation — a 7-float scale-free vector, per agent:

own_vxown_vy flow_dxflow_dylog_goal_dist local_densitynear_dx

Action

[vx, vy] continuous, clipped to ±V_MAX = 1.34 m/step (human walking speed).

Reward verbatim PRD

+10 reach goal · −5 step on a UTCI>26 °C cell · −2 per neighbour <0.5 m · + potential-based goal-progress shaping (policy-invariant, Ng 1999).

04The fix that made it work on the real site

V1 fed the policy the raw goal offset — unbounded, up to 600 m on the real venue. The policy never trained on values that big → out-of-distribution → only 0.8% of agents reached their stage.

V2 replaced it with a bounded, scale-free cue:

# raw, unbounded — the OOD trap goal_dx, goal_dy → up to ±600 # bounded flow-field cue flow_dx, flow_dy ∈ [−1, 1] log_goal_dist log-compressed

"Train local, deploy global": the policy's whole job shrinks to local collision/congestion physics — which is scale-free — so a tiny corridor transfers to the 834 m site by construction.

05The router: a BFS flow field

The agent doesn't navigate the whole venue. A deterministic BFS wavefront from each stage precomputes, per cell, a unit vector "this way to the stage" + a geodesic distance — routed around the 11.5k impassable building cells.

gate spawn→ read local flow vector→ policy picks speed→ Weidmann caps it→ stage

Global routing = deterministic flow field. Local physics = learned policy. Clean separation — the policy never has to solve the maze.

06The physics: Weidmann speed–density law

The policy wants a velocity; physics caps it. Each agent's max forward speed falls with its local crowd density along the cited Weidmann/NKW curve — so it can't teleport through a packed crowd.

v(ρ) = V0·(1 − e^(−γ(1/ρ − 1/ρ_max))) V0=1.34 m/s · ρ_max=5.4 · γ=1.913

07Training: a 3-strip curriculum

No full-site episodes — three tiny synthetic strips, ~30 min CPU, additive.

1 · Flow-follow 120k

Open field, goals in 4 directions, 40 streaming agents. Teaches the policy to follow the flow vector wherever it points.

2 · Elbow 100k

One L-bend obstacle. The flow vector rotates +x→+y, so the policy learns to track a turning cue around buildings.

3 · Corridor FD 50k

Periodic strip, density 16→260 agents. Re-asserts the Weidmann speed–density physics so the FD gate stays green.

Policy = 64×64 MLP · 138 KB · committed to git (no LFS) · seed 1789 logged as provenance.

08Runtime on the real venue

  • 650 agents spawn at two real gates — A (general, 85%) / B (VIP, 15%) — over a staggered 300-step window, so flow reads as real entrance highways, not one pulse.
  • Demand-weighted goals — each agent assigned a stage by capacity-class × headliner popularity (from the digitized 2026 line-up).
  • Buildings are impassable — any agent landing in a blocked cell is reverted every step (PRD literal).
  • 1400 deterministic steps (sized to the measured 766 m farthest-stage geodesic). Same seed → byte-identical field.

09From trajectories to the density field

1400×650×4 trajectory→ snap to UTCI 6 m lattice→ DBSCAN dwell zones→ flow vectors→ crowd_grid.json

Output is validated against a frozen JSON-Schema contract and snapped byte-exact to the heat lattice — the greedy/MCLP core consumes it with zero changes.

Provenance: crowd_density is tagged DERIVED — but only after the Weidmann benchmark passed, bound to the policy's SHA-256 (spoof-proof).

10Two gates — one green, one open

PASS Weidmann FD ±20%

The physics is validated — emergent speed–density reproduces the cited pedestrian curve at three densities. crowd_density earned DERIVED on this.

OPEN Stage-reach ≥70%

Measured 54.8% (356/650). 14 of 19 stages hit 95–100%; the deficit is 3 far stages at ~0%. Routing stays labelled ASSUMED-grade until fixed.

Diagnosed cause: a lateral dispersal force built for the FD measurement corridor leaks into the live site and mills the long-haul streams to far stages. A fix is staged; the rollout adjudicates next.
